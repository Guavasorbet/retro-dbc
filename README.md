# retro-dbc
Retro- DBC

So often I find myself in social and networking events, chatting and connecting with people. Often than not, when one exits the conversation, we find ourselves struggling to establish that line of direct connection. We pull our business cards out of our pockets, folded and wrinkled, simply reaching in just to realize I ran out of business cards.

Here's when my Bob the Builder persona kicks in and I come up with solutions. In this instance, the predicament I had was "hey, let's stay in touch" far more smoothly and, if possible, quickly. 

So DIY it is.

I bought a NFC programmable card for a whopping 0.39 cents. 
Before writing the program, I worked on the design, style, and overall purpose. I thought to myself, "I want to make a digital business card that encompasses everything that I am in a nutshell". The idea started taking shape as I blended elements of some of the most iconic fictional works such as Neuromancer by William Gibson, Tron (1982), and Escape from New York's (1981) mapping scene towards the beginning of the film.

Then I wrote, refined, and tested the program using tools such as Claude, Gemini, Adobe Cloud Lightroom, NFC app, and a headshot of myself.
Once I was pleased with the overall UI design, I stored the files on GitHub, deployed the page, and wrote and locked the URL into the card using the mobile NFC app. 

The final product is a fun project that I can utilize at any event to express who I am and give a glimpse of my personality apparatus which is       " Creative, Business-minded, people-centered, resourceful," with a heavy dose of nerdiness.
